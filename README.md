# ROOTS — שורשים

Public browser build of ROOTS, version 3.6.

Play: https://robinshmueli.github.io/roots-survival-play/

Choose a saved world or create a named world with a size and difficulty. Progress saves in the same browser. New worlds start with a clear building area and medium size selected by default.

Controls: WASD to move, Shift to sprint, mouse to aim, click or Space to use the held item or eat, E to collect and interact, C for the bag, F to craft, R to rotate a building preview, M for the map.

Craft a sword, hunt cows and chickens, and cook their meat in a wood-fueled furnace. Animals are passive during the day and hostile at night. Day lasts six minutes, evening one minute and night three minutes. Fiber bushes regrow each night.

Build cabins with walls, doors, floors and roofs. Open door leaves are passable, and nearby doors open without backing away. Trees and buildings fade while obscuring the player. Use an axe to pack and relocate the tent; the home pin follows it accurately. Sprint for twenty seconds and recover stamina in five. Hunger above 85% restores one health every three seconds.
